Water where it shouldn't be? Do this first.

Water damage gets worse by the hour. These four steps limit it while we're on the way.

  1. Shut off the main

    Find your main water shutoff, usually where the line enters the house, and turn it clockwise until it stops.

  2. Turn off the water heater

    Set a gas heater to "pilot," or flip the breaker for an electric one, so it doesn't run dry.

  3. Drain the lines

    Open the lowest faucet in the house to relieve pressure and empty what's left in the pipes.

  4. Call us

    Take photos for insurance, move valuables away from the water, and give us a call.
